Customer Social Responsibility
20
More than PLN 5.3 million – this is how much we donated in 2020 for combating the coronavirus 
pandemic
•
Since March 2020, financial and material donations have been received by 51 hospitals and treatment 
centers across Poland. In November and December alone, that was nearly PLN 850 thousand for 13 
centers
•
This aid gifted through the ENEA Foundation and LW Bogdanka is used, among others, to purchase 
medical equipment and necessary sanitary and personal protection products
•
This support is provided to medical centers operating in the territory where the Enea Group operates. 
They included, among others, medical centers in Białystok, Busko-Zdrój, Chodzież, Poznań, Radom, 
Szczecin or Zwoleń
•
The list of medical institutions receiving the support is developed in cooperation with the Minister of Health 
and the Ministry of State Assets
Mission: Prevention. Health begins in your head
•
It is a preventive health care project for Enea Group employees, which was launched in 2018 and run 
by the Enea Foundation
•
Last year in November, employees attended a meeting with specialists in the field of neuroscience and 
psychodietetics and learned how to reduce stress at work and deal with the negative effects of the 
pandemic
•
As part of the Mission: Prevention campaign, a series devoted to healthy spine and correct posture 
during remote working was also carried out

Customer Social Responsibility
21
More than 75 liters of blood were collected in 2020 as part of the “Energy in Our Blood” program
•
This project is carried out in cooperation with the Regional Blood Donation and Hemotherapy Centers.
Since the beginning of the campaign, nearly 275 liters of blood was collected in the Enea Group
•
What is important, in addition to the Group’s employees, their families and people from outside the Group, 
such as uniformed services, have become involved in the blood collection drives
•
This year the activity was extended to include information campaigns were conducted to encourage 
employees to turn up for vaccinations against COVID-19 and to donate plasma by individuals 
who had been infected with the SARS-CoV-2 virus
The Enea Group continues to engage in activities to promote safety, education, culture and sport among 
local communities
•
The Group provided financial support to the “naGranie nad Rusałką” project – a series of outdoor concerts by 
young bands from Poznań, which was broadcast on the Internet
•
As part of employee volunteerism, employees joined in the “Buy for your loved ones, help everyone” 
Christmas campaign.At a traditional charity fair, this time using a remote formula, employees 
purchased Christmas decorations made by participants of occupational therapy workshops 
for people with intellectual disabilities in Poznań, Piła and Police. Proceeds from the fair were 
used to support medical treatment of individuals taken care of by these institutions
