Assumptions
•
CDS calculations for a unit with 36% efficiency and 93% emissions indicator,
CSS calculations for a unit with 60% efficiency and 35% emissions indicator.
•
CDS and CSS calculations do not take into account additional foreign exchange
costs related to the purchase of currency on the due date or other additional
costs related to CO₂ purchases.
•
The costs of coal transport and gas transmission have been omitted.
CDS and CSS forward 
[PLN/MWh]
CDS and CSS forwards have been on an upward trend since the beginning of the
year. After declines in April and May, in June they returned to an upward trend,
which was more pronounced for CSS. In the last two months, the prices of ARA
coal and gas have been falling, while the prices of CO₂ emission allowances and
electricity have gone up. The increase in energy prices amid falling gas prices
contributed to a significant improvement in the CSS. The persisting high prices
of CO₂ emission allowances do not allow CDS values to improve and contribute
to increases in energy prices.
